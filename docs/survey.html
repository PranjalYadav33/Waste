CHAPTER 3 · SECTION P-S (PRIMARY DATA, N=100)

Primary survey results

Instrument: Google Form, form-1 - Form Responses 1.csv, administered 2 October 2026, 13:55–14:07. Housing composition: Gated Society 40; Standalone Building or Chawl 33; Other 27. With N=100, each respondent represents exactly one percentage point; figures below report exact counts.

P-S1 · Housing composition and bin ownership (Q1–Q2)

Only 19 respondents report always maintaining separate wet and dry bins; 28 do so intermittently and 53 use a single bin. Single-bin use exceeds 40% within every housing stratum, indicating that the deficit is systemic rather than confined to one dwelling type.

P-S2 · Reasons for non-segregation and collection reliability (Q3–Q4)

Stated reasons distribute as: absence of separate bins 30; lack of kitchen space 24; belief that collectors remix waste regardless 23; forgetfulness 23. Collection reliability: very regular 14; delayed by 1–2 hours 34; irregular 26; no door-to-door pickup 26. Only 14% report fully regular service.

P-S3 · Vehicle design and observed handler remixing (Q5–Q6)

67 respondents report a single-chamber collection vehicle; 21 report compartmentalised vehicles and 12 are uncertain. 60 respondents report frequently observing handlers remixing segregated bags, 27 occasionally, and 13 never — 87% combined. Among the 19 always-segregating households, 13 (68.4%) nevertheless report frequent remixing downstream, which directly attenuates the incentive to segregate at source.

P-S4 · Hazardous waste handling, penalty awareness and arrival alerts (Q7–Q9)

80 respondents dispose of hazardous and sanitary waste (pads, diapers, glass, medicines) mixed into ordinary wet (42) or dry (38) bins; 20 maintain a separate bag. 56 respondents are aware of municipal fines or bye-laws. 60 respondents state that an SMS or WhatsApp alert 15 minutes before the vehicle's arrival would assist timely handover.

P-S5 · Motivators and housing cross-tabulation (Q10)

Preferred motivators: provision of better bins 28; clearer instructions 26; strict fines 24; lower municipal maintenance fee 22. No single instrument commands a majority, which supports a combined hardware–instruction–incentive design. Single-bin use by stratum: Gated 23/40 (57.5%); Chawl 14/33 (42.4%); Other 16/27 (59.3%). Chawl respondents report the highest rate of absent door-to-door pickup, 14/33 (42.4%). Always-segregating shares are Gated 27.5%, Chawl 18.2% and Other 7.4%; downstream remixing nevertheless erases the gated lead.
